FIELD GUIDE

Defend the Tree of Life.

  1. Plant. Choose a defender and tap a glowing site. Sap pays for planting; defeated pests and cleared waves earn more.
  2. Prepare. Choose a rarity style. Tap a planted tower to buy a level upgrade with preview TREE.
  3. Defend. Send a wave. Defenders attack automatically; place more towers or use supplies during battle.
  4. Survive. Finish ten waves and defeat the Blight King. After defeat, a TREE continue restores health while preserving your wave and towers.

Keyboard: 1–5 choose defenders · N sends a wave · Space pauses · Esc clears selection.

Progress saves locally. Reloading or switching tabs pauses battle. Preview prices and balancing are provisional. No paid admission or separate season-pass purchase.

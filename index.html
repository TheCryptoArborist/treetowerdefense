FIELD GUIDE

Command the forest.

  1. Plant. Pick a guardian and tap a glowing site. Earned Sap pays for planting.
  2. Grow. Select a planted guardian. TREE upgrades grow its entire species permanently: Sapling → Guardian → Ancient. Existing and future defenders of that species benefit.
  3. Defend. Send a wave. Guardians attack automatically. Oak splashes, Pine fires needles, Palm slows, Cypress pierces, and Mushroom poisons.
  4. Build your forest. Beat ten waves to earn 1–3 stars, build a landmark, and unlock the next chapter. Stars depend on remaining health: 80+ earns three, 40+ earns two.
  5. Recover. After defeat, a 20,000 TREE continue restores health and preserves the current wave and towers. A fresh run is included; permanent growth and forest progress stay.

Keyboard: 1–5 choose defenders · N sends a wave · Space pauses · Esc clears selection.

All TREE prices and ownership are simulated. Reloading or switching tabs pauses battle. Rarity changes appearance only. No admission fee or separate season-pass purchase.

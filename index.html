FIELD GUIDE
Command the forest.
  1. Plant. Start with Oak and Pine. Clear waves 2, 4, and 6 in Emerald Crossing to earn Palm, Cypress, and Mushroom. Pick an unlocked defender and tap a glowing site. Earned Sap pays for planting.
  2. Grow. Select a planted guardian. TREE upgrades one numbered defender permanently: Sapling → Guardian → Ancient. Other defenders keep their own levels. Choose a copy in your collection before deploying it; Sap pays for deployment.
  3. Defend. Send a wave. Guardians attack automatically. Select a planted guardian to choose First (furthest along), Strongest (most health remaining), or Fastest (current movement speed). Orders apply to that guardian and are included. Try Fastest on Palm for moths; keep some guardians on First to catch escapes. Oak splashes, Pine fires needles, Palm slows, Cypress pierces, and Mushroom poisons. Willow chains pulses between nearby pests. The Watchtower ignores beetle armor; the Cannon bombards clusters.
  4. Build your forest. Beat ten waves to earn 1–3 stars, build a landmark, and unlock the next chapter. Stars depend on remaining health: 80+ earns three, 40+ earns two. Protect Emerald Crossing to earn the Archer Watchtower, earn 4 total stars for Willow, and protect Sunpetal Meadow for the Sap Cannon. Unlocks stay across runs; they grant new roles. TREE still pays for upgrades, supplies, and continues.
  5. Recover. After defeat, a 20,000 TREE continue restores health and preserves the current wave and towers. A fresh run is included; permanent growth and forest progress stay.
Keyboard: 1–8 choose defenders · N sends a wave · Space pauses · Esc clears selection.
TREE balances and prices are simulated. NFTree access is verified. Reloading or switching tabs pauses battle. Rarity changes appearance only. No admission fee or separate season-pass purchase.

# Christmas Chaos — development preview

Target launch: **November 27, 2026**, the day after Thanksgiving. This draft is separate from the live Harvest release. The public mode chooser still shows the Christmas teaser; `startEvent('christmas-chaos')` remains closed. The explicit `startChristmasPreview()` engine method is used only by local development tests and recording fixtures, after the normal engine access and Emerald-completion checks. No public entry, authenticated player session, token transfer or hosted deployment is created here.

## Winter mode

A dedicated frozen switchback has twelve new build positions, snowy forest terrain and textured ice lanes. New invaders are Ribbon Mites, Frost Bauble Beetles, Frostwing Moths, Holly Blight and the Tinsel Tyrant. Defenses retain the original sprites, faces, limb proportions and aim sockets. A cached winter foliage palette, red ribbons, gifts, pad lights and Tree of Life lights create the seasonal uniform; no floating head accessories or face overlays are used.

**Frost armor** absorbs ordinary direct damage before health is lost. Bauble Beetles start with 40 armor; the Tyrant has 1,650 health and 150 armor. Watchtower, Cypress, poison and the existing Leaf Storm bypass frost. Poison also retains its usual regeneration counter.

**Canopy Bell** is free once per wave while pests are on the path and the battle is running. It clears current frost armor, stuns pests for 2.5 battle seconds and gives defenders 25% faster reloads for six battle seconds. It spends neither Sap nor TREE. Pause, reload and continue preserve use and remaining timers; the next wave refreshes the charge.

**Tyrant blizzard** can occur once below half health, adding 40 frost armor to all living invaders. Keeping the bell for this moment is an alternative to piercing coverage and poison. This differs from Harvest's seed-cache raids, repeating tailwinds and boss escorts.

## Progress and preview

Winter progress is separate from Campaign, Veteran and Harvest. The engine records four cosmetic reward milestones: completion, 80+ health, bell use in three waves of a completed run, and 100 health. Cosmetic equipment UI and the player-facing winter entry are still to be integrated before release. No prizes or token rewards are introduced.

The [12-second preview](preview.mp4) is genuine offline engine playback of the final wave, including its naturally triggered blizzard and the bell response 1.3 seconds later. It uses earned level-one defenders and zero TREE spending. The [poster](poster.jpg) and [verification record](verification.json) were generated by `tools/render-christmas-preview.mjs`; they are not hosted holder screenshots.

Validation: 333 tests passed, one Windows-only skip; syntax and protected-build checks passed. The five-wave earned-roster simulation finishes at 100 health without upgrades or purchases. Eight new tests cover preview-only entry, frost absorption/counters, bell timing/reload/pause/continue, one-time blizzard persistence, reward isolation, malformed saves, full-run balance and actual reload acceleration. The art audit preserves alpha geometry and 131 neutral dark face pixels while recoloring 555 foliage pixels.

Next release work: wire the bell and winter readouts into the holder UI; author winter story scenes; implement and verify the November 27 entry activation; complete desktop/phone playtests. Existing live release records remain authoritative until a later verified deployment.

## Artwork

New assets, generated with the built-in image tool and encoded as WebP at quality 86:

- `assets/environment/terrain-winter-v1.webp`: a birds-eye snowy Christmas forest clearing, blue slate and frost, open central ground, fir/holly/lantern/gift details around the border; no authored roads, characters, text or UI. Original forest terrain supplied as style reference.
- `assets/enemies/christmas-invaders-v1.webp`: transparent 3×2 top-down atlas, one upward-facing full-body insect per cell: ribbon-and-gift mite; icy red/gold Bauble Beetle; holly-marked Frostwing Moth; thorn-and-berry Holly Blight; antlered clockwork Tinsel Tyrant; thawed version of the Bauble Beetle. Mature painterly texture, intact separated silhouettes, no human faces, text or connecting shadows.

Original generated PNGs remain in the generation output folder. Winter assets have a separate opt-in loader and are not requested by the ordinary game's existing loading entrance.

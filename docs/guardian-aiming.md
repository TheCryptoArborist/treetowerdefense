# Guardian aiming · v0.7

Guardians previously used one front-facing growth portrait on the battlefield, and every projectile began at the same offset above the build site. Their bodies therefore did not face approaching pests, and needles appeared to emerge through the side of a figure.

Battlefield guardians now choose one of eight upright views toward their actual target. Each species has five painted views at each of its three growth levels: front (S), front-right (SE), right (E), rear-right (NE), and back (N). Mirroring the three right views supplies SW, W, and NW. The original portrait/growth atlases remain unchanged for planting cards and inspectors. Eight discrete directions are used rather than a continuous skeletal turn animation.

## Assets and launch points

| Species | New transparent atlas | Launch point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pine | `assets/defenders/pine-aim-v1.png` | Needle-launcher gauntlet tips |
| Oak | `assets/defenders/oak-aim-v1.png` | Roots at the build site; traveling ground shockwave |
| Palm | `assets/defenders/palm-aim-v1.png` | Leaf-fan staff |
| Cypress | `assets/defenders/cypress-aim-v1.png` | Spear tip |
| Mushroom | `assets/defenders/mushroom-aim-v1.png` | Open casting palm |

Each original 1619×971 PNG is preserved unchanged. Rows are Sapling, Guardian, and Ancient; columns are S, SE, E, NE, and N. Generated spacing is approximate, so the runtime isolates the 15 connected bodies and their antialiased edges instead of cutting a strict grid. This preserves extended weapons crossing nominal cell boundaries and excludes neighboring characters. Crop-sized intermediate canvases avoid allocating fifteen full atlas canvases. Every growth row shares one scale across its views. Recolors are cached once for all six cosmetic appearances.

The launch coordinates in `src/art.js` were calibrated separately against each species, growth row, and painted view. The same crop/scale/flip transform positions both the guardian and its launch point. Projectiles pin their origin when created; the guardian holds that facing until the projectile expires. A small launch pulse clarifies the casting point. Idle bobbing stops during that hold so the weapon does not drift away from its pinned origin. Left views mirror the launch point along with the figure. Missing art uses a directional native-canvas fallback with matching casting points.

## Presentation boundaries

- `Game.targetsFor()` shares the existing range calculation and furthest-along-route priority with presentation tracking. It sorts a filtered array and does not mutate enemy order.
- Actual attacks emit a transient event with tower ID, target ID/coordinates, species, growth, and style before damage resolution. The new `GuardianAiming` controller locks that attack direction, even if the target dies or another pest becomes the next priority.
- The presentation owns at most 64 short-lived projectile visuals. The last killing shot can finish after the engine clears the wave's shots. Damage still resolves immediately as before; these projectiles do not introduce travel-time damage or change attack intervals.
- Facing and shot timers stop during pause, dialogs, and hidden tabs. New runs, chapter changes, and Preview Reset clear them. Removed tower poses are pruned. A loaded paused battle derives facing from saved enemies; aiming effects never enter saves.
- Reduced motion preserves the directional view and projectile explanation, with no idle bob or whole-figure recoil.
- Combat stats, prices, target priority, waves, Sap/TREE accounting, rarity entitlements, permanent growth, and v1/v2 save migration are unchanged. Future structure towers remain gallery concepts. Ownership and payments remain simulated.

- All five atlases decode and isolate 75 complete poses. Native canvas renders all 720 species × growth × appearance × direction combinations, plus the same 720 fallback combinations, without errors. Draw functions do not mutate shot inputs.
- Launch-point sheets for all growth rows and eight directions were visually inspected, including mirrored views, gauntlet tips, staff fans, spear tips, and casting palms.
- Local HTTP asset routes serve the five new atlases and aiming module with correct content types. Non-game files and unknown atlases remain unavailable.

## Manual playtest

1. Plant Pine at sites on opposite sides of the route. Watch a pest approach from the right, pass below or above the guardian, and move left. Confirm front, side, rear, and mirrored views switch toward the target and needles start at the gauntlet.
2. Repeat with Palm, Cypress, and Mushroom; inspect staff, spear, and palm origins. Oak should turn toward its target and send its attack along the ground from its roots.
3. Upgrade each species through Guardian and Ancient. Check launch alignment at all growth levels and with each eligible rarity appearance. Confirm costs and permanent progress remain as before.
4. Pause during a shot, open a purchase review during battle, and switch tabs. Confirm the firing pose and projectile wait; resume and watch the same shot finish.
5. Watch the final killing shot of a wave. It should finish after the wave-clear notice rather than vanish. Start a new run or change chapters; no old shots should remain.
6. Reload during battle. Confirm the game restores paused with the same purchased growth, balance, and enemies, and derives a sensible facing without replaying old attacks.
7. Repeat in a narrow viewport and with reduced motion. Direction changes and launch points should remain readable.

## Generation method and final prompts

Method: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, five separate calls, `transparent_background: true`. Each call supplied its existing `assets/defenders/{species}-warriors.png` growth atlas as the character reference. Outputs were copied into the repository unchanged. The requested 2560×1536 layout yielded 1619×971 outputs; connected-body isolation handles the loose spacing. No external font, image service, or new runtime package is used by the game.

These are the complete final prompts used for the five outputs.

### Pine

Output: `assets/defenders/pine-aim-v1.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent directional attack-pose atlas for an existing tower-defense game. Input image 1 is the EXACT character and three growth-stage design reference, not an edit target. Preserve the jagged stump head, black hollow eyes and shouting mouth/teeth where the face is visible, bark body and limbs, root boots, leaf shoots, equipment and reference identity. Generate new poses of this same character rather than a new design. Crisp game sprites with bold dark outlines and textured shaded bark, serious expressive warriors, no cute smiling tree. Layout: a wide 2560 by 1536 canvas, STRICT REGULAR GRID of FIVE equal-width columns and THREE equal-height rows: exactly FIFTEEN separate complete full-body figures, one per cell, never touching. Leave transparent gutters at every cell boundary, keep shields/weapons/leaves inside their own cell, feet centered horizontally on each cell's 88%-height baseline. Rows TOP TO BOTTOM: Sapling (reference left, plain compact equipment), Guardian (reference middle, shoulder armor/reinforced shield), Ancient (reference right, heavy elaborate bark armor). Each row repeats the same growth-stage character at FIVE camera-relative orientations; columns LEFT TO RIGHT: 1 FRONT facing SCREEN DOWN/SOUTH toward viewer; 2 FRONT-RIGHT THREE-QUARTER facing SCREEN LOWER-RIGHT/SOUTHEAST; 3 RIGHT PROFILE facing SCREEN RIGHT/EAST; 4 REAR-RIGHT THREE-QUARTER facing SCREEN UPPER-RIGHT/NORTHEAST, with back of torso/head prominent; 5 FULL BACK facing SCREEN UP/NORTH, no face visible. This is a real rotation around an upright character's vertical axis, never tilting or rotating the whole sprite on the canvas. Use an elevated game camera; head, body, feet and attack equipment all face the listed direction. All fifteen are braced attack-ready poses; the arm/equipment aims in the facing direction. Hands at lower chest height. Keep lighting, scale within each row, perspective and anatomical identity consistent across the five views. TRUE transparent background, no background gradients, no glow behind characters, no ground scene, no labels, no text, no UI, no frame, no watermark, no extra figures. Subject: Pine warrior: honey-brown stump, narrow dark-green pine needles, wooden forearm needle-launcher gauntlet, small wooden shield. Extend the needle-launcher arm in the facing direction, muzzle visibly pointed at the enemy. Do not point the muzzle sideways away from the facing direction.
```

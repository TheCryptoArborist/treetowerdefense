# The Severed Root — illustrated wave cutscenes

An industrial blight tries to sever the three forest junctions feeding the Tree of Life. Emerald exposes a deliberate assault, Sunpetal reveals the seed-vault threat, and Moonlit reconnects the oldest roots. Ironroot Tree Bot, Chainsaw Raider and Blight Sprayer are woven into their actual encounter points. Veteran revisits describe the returning reinforced assault. Harvest and legacy Haunted Grove have separate five-wave arcs. Old runs without encounters use alternate text at mini-boss checkpoints.

The native modal now uses a twelve-second, three-shot animated scene drawn by the actual game painter: layered forest/camera movement, entering guardians and enemies, hand-connected attacks, root signals and a final guardian framing. Chapter lighting, mini-boss warnings and renewal endings follow the story. Each checkpoint retains two manually advanced text beats: narration and guardian dialogue, followed by a real next-wave forecast. See [November 5 playtest corrections](playtest-corrections.md) for the animation/reference details and validation. No voices are included.

The opening plays when the player explicitly sends wave 1; dismissing/skipping that prologue starts that requested wave. Subsequent cutscenes play automatically on wave-clear events, including the ending; they return to build/results without starting the next wave. A weak-map per-run checkpoint latch prevents duplicate draws from reopening scenes. Reloaded ongoing runs are not restarted for story. The map's Story button replays the last completed checkpoint (never the current live wave’s ending); a running replay explicitly pauses the game and remains paused afterward.

Skip scene and Escape dismiss the current scene. An independent local preference disables future automatic scenes, while Story remains available. Native dialog focus/inert behavior, labeled checkbox, autofocus, scrolling phone layout and reduced-motion camera support are included. Simulation, aiming and combat effect clocks hold while the modal is open; keyboard, Shop and management entry are blocked. Story data and preferences are not payment entitlements or ownership proof. No automatic spending, rewards, stat changes, paid unlocks or blockchain calls are added.
